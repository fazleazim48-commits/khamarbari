# Khramar Bari Vision

Create a complete, modern, premium Bengali website for a brand called **“খামার বাড়ি”**.

## BRAND & BUSINESS

**Brand Name:** খামার বাড়ি

**Owner:** Fazle Elahi Nasir

**Address:** Shopura, Lokkhon Pur, Monohorgonj, Cumilla, Bangladesh

**Mobile:** 01819929185

“খামার বাড়ি” is an innovative integrated farming business focused on poultry and fish farming. The main concept is a large raised/floating poultry farming structure positioned above a water area, while fish farming is operated underneath and around the water area.

The business also sells high-quality poultry feed and fish feed.

The website must be completely in **Bangla**. All headings, descriptions, buttons, navigation items, product information, labels and other visible website content should be written naturally in Bengali.

Do not use lorem ipsum or meaningless placeholder text. Write realistic, professional Bengali content based on the business concept.

## PROFESSIONAL LOGO

Create and use a **professional, premium logo for “খামার বাড়ি”**.

The logo should represent the brand's integrated poultry + fish farming concept in a simple and memorable way.

Logo direction:

* Bengali brand name: **খামার বাড়ি**

* Include a subtle combination of poultry/farm and fish/water elements.

* Use a clean agricultural identity.

* Avoid overly complicated illustrations.

* The logo should look professional enough for a real agricultural/farming company.

* Use deep green, natural earthy tones and subtle blue/water accents.

* Create a clean version suitable for both desktop and mobile headers.

* Also create/use a simplified icon version for favicon/social media.

* Keep the logo modern, premium and easy to recognize.

Do not make the logo look like a generic clip-art farming logo.
